## Project Description:

Our site “Sock-Azon” will allow people to easily browse, buy, or sell personalized socks of many different types. For example, we would carry options for winter socks, sports socks, luxury socks, novelty socks, kids socks, casual socks, and there are many more possibilities we have yet to explore. 
There will be three kinds of users on the website. We can have both registered and unregistered users. The unregistered users will not get full access of the website without an account and will only be able to do the following:

-Browse available products by category.
-Search for socks using keywords.
-View product details, including descriptions, prices, and images.
-Read customer reviews and ratings.
-Register an account to access additional features.

The registered users, however, will have full access to the website, being able to do the following:

-Log in and maintain an account.
-Create and edit a user profile (including name, email, and profile image).
-Add socks to a shopping cart.
-Proceed to checkout and complete purchases securely.
-Leave product reviews and rate purchases.
-Track order history and order status.
-Save favorite items for future purchases.

The final user of the website will be the admin users. This would be the people maintaining the website and processing all customer inquiries and posting all of the listings of the different kinds of socks that are being sold. Admin Users can do the following:

-Monitor user activity and manage user accounts.
-Add, edit, or remove sock listings.
-Manage customer orders and process returns.
-Respond to customer inquiries and feedback.

## Core Features and Functional Requirements:

### User Account System:

-Secure registration and login with passwords.
-Profile management for users to update personal information.

### Product Catalog and Search:

-Socks categorized for easy browsing.
-Detailed pages for the products with descriptions, pricing, and images.
-Search functionality for quick access to products.

### Shopping Cart and Checkout:

-Users can add or remove items from their cart.
-Checkout system with secure payment.
-Order confirmation and receipt sent to the user.

### Reviews and Ratings:

-Registered users can leave product reviews and ratings.
-Reviews are displayed on product pages for other shoppers.

### Order Tracking and History:

-Users can track their current orders and view past purchases.
-Admins can update order statuses.

### Admin Dashboard:

-Admins can add, edit, or remove products.
-View and manage customer orders and transactions.
-Search for users by name or email.
-Enable or disable user accounts as needed.

### Responsive and User-Friendly Design:

-Mobile-friendly layout for easy use on any device.
-Clear navigation menus for exploring the website.

### Security and Error Handling:

-JavaScript validation to prevent incorrect data entry.
-Proper error messages for bad navigation or inputs.
